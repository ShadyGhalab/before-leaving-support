What can I use ReadyKin for?

Use ReadyKin for reminders, packing, shared trips, errands, location alerts, recurring routines, pet care, plant care, and more.

Can I plan with other people?

Yes. Shared trips and groups are designed to help people assign items, follow progress, and stay on the same page.

Which Apple devices are supported?

ReadyKin is designed for iPhone and iPad, with Apple Watch and Mac support. Check the App Store compatibility section for the requirements of the version available to you.
