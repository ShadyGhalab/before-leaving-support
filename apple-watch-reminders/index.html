What can you do with ReadyKin?

ReadyKin includes Apple Watch support alongside its iPhone and iPad experience. Use the watch as a convenient companion for reminders while keeping your larger planning tasks on the device that suits them. Check the current App Store listing for supported devices and system requirements.

From idea to action

Make it part of your routine.

  1. 01

    Set up the app on your devices

    Install the current compatible version of ReadyKin and make sure the companion experience is available on your watch. Check the App Store rather than relying on an old requirements list.

  2. 02

    Prepare the plan on iPhone

    Create the reminder or checklist with clear, short item names. Do the detailed trip planning and editing where you have enough room to review everything.

  3. 03

    Check the watch experience

    Open ReadyKin on the watch and check which reminders and actions are available. Test notifications and companion behavior before relying on them for an important task.

Make the reminder easy to understand at a glance

A watch is most useful when the next action is short and specific. “Pack the charger” is clearer than a long note about all the electronics you might need. Keep extra explanation in the plan you prepare on iPhone.

For a larger trip, finish the main packing work first. Let the watch support the remaining small actions rather than expecting it to replace every planning screen.

Compatibility is a current-version question

Device requirements can change as the app develops. Check the App Store compatibility section for the version available to you, and review the watch companion settings on your own devices.

If the watch does not show what you expect, make a note of your iPhone model, watch model, operating system versions and ReadyKin version. Those details make it easier for support to investigate.

Good to know

  • Apple Watch is a companion experience. This page does not promise every iPhone feature works independently on the watch.
  • Compatibility, notification delivery and synchronization depend on your devices and settings. Verify them before relying on an important reminder.

Your questions, answered.

Does ReadyKin support Apple Watch?

Yes. ReadyKin includes Apple Watch support. Check the current App Store listing for compatibility and the companion options available in your version.

Is every iPhone feature available on Apple Watch?

Do not assume full feature parity. Use iPhone or iPad for detailed planning and check the actions supported by the watch app.

Where can I check the minimum system version?

The App Store compatibility section is the place to check the requirements of the current release.

What should I send support about a watch issue?

Include your iPhone and Apple Watch models, their operating system versions, your ReadyKin version and a description of what you expected and what happened.
