ReadyKin · formerly Before Leaving

What can you do with ReadyKin?

ReadyKin is a packing list app for iPhone that brings trip preparation, reminders and shared planning together. Instead of keeping a list in one place and remembering last-minute tasks somewhere else, you can organize the trip and the things you need around it. ReadyKin was previously called Before Leaving.

From idea to action

Make it part of your routine.

  1. 01

    Start with your trip

    Create your trip in ReadyKin and add the details you already know. Think about how long you will be away, what you plan to do and who is coming.

  2. 02

    Build a practical list

    Add essentials, use packing suggestions where available and edit the result. Include the items you actually use rather than accepting every possible suggestion.

  3. 03

    Check what is ready

    Mark items as packed as you prepare. Give last-minute items, such as a charger or passport, a separate reminder so they are not lost in the rest of the list.

A packing list and a reminder solve different problems

A packing list tells you what belongs in the bag. A reminder tells you when to deal with something that is not ready yet. You may pack clothes the night before, but still need your charging phone until you leave.

Use the trip list for the complete picture and a timed reminder for the final tasks. For anything that must happen before departure, choose a time that leaves enough room to act.

Start small, then make it yours

A useful first list can be as simple as documents, clothing, toiletries, electronics and the things needed for your activities. Remove items you do not need and add the details that make the trip different from your last one.

For a shared trip, decide who is responsible for common items. One person can bring the adapter while another brings the shared charger. The goal is less duplication, not a longer list.

Good to know

  • Packing suggestions are a starting point. Review the list for your own needs and travel requirements.
  • Feature availability and paid options can vary. Check the current App Store listing and the app before choosing a plan.

A practical starting point

Example: a two-night city break

Start with this short list, then adapt it to your plans. This is an example, not a generated forecast or an exhaustive travel checklist.

  • Travel documents and booking details
  • Outfits for your plans and comfortable shoes
  • Toiletries you use every day
  • Phone, charger and the right adapter
  • A layer or rain protection after checking the forecast
  • A reminder for the items you use until departure

Your questions, answered.

Can I use ReadyKin as an iPhone packing list app?

Yes. ReadyKin includes trip planning and packing lists so you can organize essentials and check items off as you prepare.

Can I combine a packing list with reminders?

Yes. Use the packing list for what needs to come with you and reminders for tasks or items that need attention at a particular time or place.

Can other people help with trip preparation?

ReadyKin supports shared trips and checklists. Invite the people you are traveling with and agree who is responsible for shared items.

Is Before Leaving the same app as ReadyKin?

Yes. Before Leaving is the former name of ReadyKin. The App Store app ID remains 719622441.
