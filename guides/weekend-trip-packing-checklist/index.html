A practical ReadyKin guide

A weekend packing checklist you can actually finish.

Use a practical weekend-trip packing checklist for documents, clothes, toiletries and last-minute items. Check it on this page, copy it or print it.

Start with what your plans require, not everything you own. This example is designed for a short trip and is deliberately small. Adapt it to your activities, luggage and personal needs. It is a planning template, not travel advice or a complete list for every traveler.

Reduce duplicates before adding extras

Review the list once you have gathered the main items. Can the same layer work for two activities? Has someone else agreed to bring the shared adapter? Remove duplicates you do not need, but keep the essentials that are specific to your own trip.

Give late items their own reminder

A checklist is easy to finish too early when the phone is still charging or an item is still in use. Keep those items unchecked and give yourself a time-based reminder before departure. The final check should confirm what is actually with you, not simply what you planned to pack.
