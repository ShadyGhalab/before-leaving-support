A practical ReadyKin guide

A calmer final check before you leave home.

Try a short leaving-home checklist for keys, phone, your bag and last-minute tasks. Use the browser checklist, copy it or print your own version.

A final check works best when it is short and specific. Use this example to make sure the things you need are actually with you. Replace anything that does not fit your day. This checklist is an organizational aid, not a guarantee of home safety or a substitute for your own judgment.

Prepare earlier, check later

Do most preparation before the final few minutes. Put useful items together and give unfinished tasks an earlier reminder. Keep the departure checklist for the things that are easy to miss at the last moment.

Use a reminder before the moment you need it

Set a timed reminder with enough time to respond. Do not assume that a location departure alert will appear while you are still inside. For important tasks, check directly rather than relying on any one notification.
