# ReadyKin: website discovery and App Store ASO

## What is implemented

The site makes one product entity explicit, supplies useful text pages around different use cases, exposes canonical URLs and crawlable navigation, preserves the old domain, and uses descriptive titles/social cards/App Store links. The eight pages are not “best app” listicles that falsely rank their owner above competitors. The two checklist guides provide actual standalone utility.

No hidden AI instructions, keyword-stuffed footers, fabricated reviews, automatic doorway pages, fake competitor comparisons, or special “rank me first” files are added. Google states that its AI features do not require special AI text files or a special schema. OpenAI documents OAI-SearchBot access, not a paid guaranteed organic placement mechanism. See SOURCES.md.

## Immediate account work: actual App Store ASO

The repository cannot edit App Store Connect metadata. Apple permits up to 30 characters for name and subtitle and explains keywords, screenshots and product-page optimization in its official product-page guidance. Use verified query/conversion data, not a website title as proof of an optimal App Store keyword.

A **candidate to test**, not automatically applied or claimed proven:

- Name: `ReadyKin: Reminders & Packing`
- Subtitle: `Shared lists, trips & routines`

Both are within 30 characters. Keep the current name instead when existing brand/conversion data favors it. Review naming availability and the current app's focus. Localize naturally; do not copy English keyword lists into every locale.

A candidate screenshot sequence: the core “remember before leaving” outcome, a reminder example, a trip packing list, shared preparation, then the in-app assistant. Use real current UI and accurate availability/paywall language. Test a coherent visual/message change in App Store Connect rather than attributing any install increase to SEO automatically. Do not include competing brands in the keyword field or describe medical efficacy.

Support, marketing and privacy URLs should point to the canonical public pages in OFFICIAL_LINKS.md. Exact current pricing, languages, seller and version should come from the actual store records.

## Original content, not thin expansion

Maintain these pages as distinct use cases:

| Page | Primary problem |
|---|---|
| packing-list-app | Building and reusing a practical iPhone trip packing list |
| location-reminders | Remembering something on arrival/departure |
| family-packing-list | Coordinating personal and shared family items |
| ai-packing-list | Turning an in-app prompt into a reviewed packing draft |
| weather-packing-list | Reviewing packing suggestions against conditions |
| before-leaving-checklist | Repeating a short leaving-home check |
| shared-travel-checklist | Planning a trip with another person |

Do not multiply pages for dozens of destinations unless each includes substantial original helpful content. Only add another language when the complete page and navigation are translated and maintained; then add matching self/reciprocal hreflang and canonical URLs.

## Third-party editorial outreach

Start with existing independent iOS reviewers, family-travel writers and packing/productivity coverage that genuinely matches the feature. Read their policy and contact method. Offer real app access and factual screenshots. Do not demand a positive review or undisclosed links; do not create fake user testimonials.

Example outreach draft:

> Subject: ReadyKin: shared packing lists and before-leaving reminders for iPhone
>
> Hello,
>
> I build ReadyKin, formerly Before Leaving. It combines reminders, trip packing lists and shared preparation on Apple devices. The use case is practical: organize what each traveler brings, then keep the before-leaving checks alongside the trip.
>
> Your coverage of [specific relevant article] made me think it could be useful to evaluate. The press page includes the current product summary and owned screenshots: https://beforeleaving.app/press.html. I would be happy to provide access and answer factual questions, with no expectation of a positive review.
>
> Best,
> Shady

Replace the bracketed reference with a real article before sending. No outreach has been sent. Third-party coverage is not guaranteed, nor is a resulting recommendation in ChatGPT.

## Recurring maintenance

Review Search Console/Bing indexing, landing-page usefulness and real store-button clicks. Update stale screenshots and changelog entries. Use the prompt benchmark as an observational test, not a public claim of an AI “rank.” Keep the same prompt cohort and record model, locale, date, search setting and repetition.
