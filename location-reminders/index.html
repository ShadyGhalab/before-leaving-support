Reminders by place

The right reminder. At the right place.

Create arrival and departure reminders in ReadyKin for iPhone. Connect a task to a place and learn when a timed reminder is the better choice.

What can you do with ReadyKin?

ReadyKin supports location-based reminders for arriving at or leaving a chosen place. Use them for tasks connected to home, work or another location. Keep a timed reminder as well when something must be done before you cross the location boundary.

From idea to action

Make it part of your routine.

  1. 01

    Choose the place

    Create a reminder and choose the location relevant to the task. Check the selected place carefully, especially when several locations have similar names.

  2. 02

    Choose arrival or departure

    Use arrival for something you can do after reaching a place. Use departure for a task associated with leaving. Add a clear action, such as “Pick up the parcel”.

  3. 03

    Check permissions and try it

    Allow the location and notification permissions requested by the app for your setup. Test the reminder on an ordinary trip before depending on it for an important task.

Arrival, departure or a specific time?

An arrival reminder can be useful for asking a question when you get to work or dealing with an errand at a destination. A departure reminder is useful when the task is connected with moving on from a place.

A passport, keys or a packed lunch should usually be checked before you leave. Set a time-based reminder early enough to act and use a before-leaving checklist for the final check. A location event should not be treated as a guaranteed warning before you walk out.

Write the action, not just the object

“Put the charger in my bag” is easier to act on than “Charger”. Add the detail you will need when the alert appears so you do not have to remember why you made it.

For a repeat routine, review the place, schedule and notification settings after making changes. If an alert is not behaving as expected, note what happened and send the details to support rather than repeatedly relying on the same setup.

Good to know

  • Location reminders depend on device settings, permissions and location detection. They are not guaranteed to trigger at an exact instant.
  • Use a time-based reminder in advance for essential before-departure checks. Do not rely on this app as a safety-critical alarm.

A practical starting point

Example: the workday handover

These are ways to think about reminder timing, not promises about exact alert delivery.

  • Before leaving: put your laptop and access card in your bag
  • At a chosen time: check the train or meeting details
  • On arrival: ask about the parcel at reception
  • On departure: start the next part of your after-work routine

Your questions, answered.

Can ReadyKin remind me when I arrive somewhere?

Yes. You can create a reminder associated with arrival at a chosen location, with the appropriate permissions and settings enabled.

Can ReadyKin remind me when I leave home?

ReadyKin supports departure reminders. For items that must already be in your bag before leaving, also set a timed reminder in advance.

Why might a location reminder not appear when I expect it?

Check the selected place, trigger and permissions, along with notification and Focus settings. Location detection and alert timing can vary. Contact support with your device and app details if the issue continues.

Does this replace an alarm for important tasks?

No. Treat location reminders as an organizational aid, not a guaranteed or safety-critical alarm.
