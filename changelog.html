These are archived entries from the previous website. They are not the current feature, language or system-requirements list. Refer to the App Store for current information.

Version 2.0 January 2026

The biggest update yet! Introducing Family Groups, Plant Tracker, Pet Care, and much more.

✨ New Features

  • Family Groups — Create groups with family, friends, or teams and share reminders in real-time
  • Home Hub — New dashboard for routines, safety checklists, pet care, and plant tracking
  • Plant Tracker — Track watering schedules, light requirements, and plant health
  • Pet Care — Manage feeding, walks, grooming, and health for your pets
  • Travel Mode — Enhanced trip planning with smart packing lists and flight tracking
  • Safety Checklist — Gamified home security checks before leaving
  • 7 Beautiful Themes — Pink, Green, Coral Reef, Cosmic Explorer, Forest Glow, Cherry Blossom, Goldfish
  • Voice Notes — Record audio notes for any reminder item
  • Live Activities — Dynamic Island and Lock Screen widgets (iPhone 14 Pro+)

🚀 Improvements

  • Enhanced reminder detail screen with interactive UI
  • Better progress tracking and saving
  • Select All / Deselect All functionality
  • Enhanced animations and haptic feedback
  • Improved localization support for all 7 languages
  • Optimized performance and reduced battery usage

🐛 Bug Fixes

  • Fixed notification delivery issues on iOS 17
  • Resolved Apple Watch sync delays
  • Fixed widget refresh issues
  • Various UI and stability improvements
