ReadyKin · formerly Before Leaving

What can you do with ReadyKin?

ReadyKin, formerly Before Leaving, helps you prepare for the moment you head out. Keep a short checklist of what belongs with you and use reminders for tasks that need to happen beforehand. Start with one routine you repeat often, such as leaving for work or the gym.

From idea to action

Make it part of your routine.

  1. 01

    Pick one departure routine

    Choose a situation you want to make easier. A workday, a gym visit and a weekend trip each need a different short list.

  2. 02

    Write a small, actionable checklist

    Name the things you can physically check: keys in hand, charger in bag or the right documents ready. Leave out items that never need checking.

  3. 03

    Give yourself time to act

    Set a reminder before the expected departure time. Check the essentials while you still have time to find or finish something.

Separate packing from the final check

Prepare most things earlier, then use the final checklist for items that are easy to miss. Your phone may be charging, your lunch may still be in the fridge and the umbrella may be by a different door.

A final check works best when it is short enough to finish. If the list is getting long, move preparation tasks to an earlier reminder rather than making departure more complicated.

Choose the reminder for the moment

A timed reminder can bring the routine to mind before you leave. A location reminder connects a task with arriving or departing, but may be too late for an item that must already be in your bag.

For essentials, combine a checklist with an advance time-based reminder. Review notification settings and try the routine on an ordinary day before relying on it for an important departure.

Good to know

  • Checklists support preparation; they do not guarantee that every task is complete or every alert will arrive on time.
  • Do not use the app as the only safeguard for medical, security or other safety-critical tasks.

A practical starting point

Example: the everyday five

Use this as a small starting point and replace items that do not belong in your day.

  • Keys and access card
  • Phone and anything that was charging
  • Wallet or the documents you need
  • Your bag and the items for today’s plans
  • Any last-minute item kept elsewhere at home

Your questions, answered.

Is ReadyKin the app that used to be called Before Leaving?

Yes. ReadyKin is the current name of Before Leaving. The existing website domain and App Store app ID connect the two names.

Can I use it for everyday routines rather than travel?

Yes. Reminders and checklists can be used for work, gym visits, errands and other preparation routines.

Should I use a departure reminder for my passport or keys?

Use an advance time-based reminder and a final checklist for items you need before going out. A departure trigger should not be assumed to arrive before you cross the door.

Where can I find a simple checklist to try?

The leaving-home guide on this website includes a short, printable example you can adapt. The website checklist is separate from the ReadyKin app.
